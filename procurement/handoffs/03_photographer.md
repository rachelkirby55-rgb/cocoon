# Handoff: Toronto photographer for the website shoot

_Status as of Oct 19, 2026._

## Requirements
- Toronto-based.
- Product/CPG experience.
- A "cool" imagery style like Mirror Water, Health Hut and Formula Fig.

## Style definition (agreed working definition)
- A warm, tonal palette that comes from the product: cream, butter, amber, terracotta.
- Hard directional sunlight with crisp shadows.
- Product in real-feeling designed sets: tile, sinks, linen, stone plinths.
- Sensory texture: water, foam, oil.
- Editorial and understated: hands and skin rather than faces.

Health Hut is a Toronto retailer whose own line comes in amber bottles similar to Cocoon's. The owner could ask them who shot it.

No photographer publishes rates, so every quote must be requested.

## Brief must cover
1. **Shot list:** per SKU, 1 hero, 2–3 product-page shots and 1 capsule macro, plus 2–3 group shots. Crops: 4:5, 1:1 and 16:9.
2. **Usage:** web, social and email for 2–3 years. Paid ads quoted separately.
3. **Styling and props:** who does them. Amber glass needs careful reflection control. No plastic or silicone props (EDC rule).
4. **Models:** hands only, or none.
