# Handoff: Branded pill organizer

_Status as of Oct 19, 2026: PAUSED by the owner._

## Requirements
- **Purpose:** branded with Cocoon, used as a gift with purchase or sold separately (~$30–60 retail).
- **Volume:** bulk, about 250–1,000 units.
- **Format:** divided by day or by time of day. Each compartment holds 4–6 size 00 capsules (23.3 × 8.5 mm).
- **Plastic-free and EDC-free:** no plastic, silicone, epoxy or glued magnets, BPA tin linings, PU/PVC, PFAS, or lead/cadmium glazes.
- **Look:** premium. The owner dislikes bamboo and wood (looks cheap).

## Decision so far
Custom CNC-machined plain aluminum case with a slide-on lid, modelled on a reference photo of a 3D-printed two-row case. Start with a **single row of 7**.

Draft spec:
- **Compartments:** ~28 × 20 mm inside, 16 mm deep.
- **Overall size:** ~156 × 32 × 22 mm.
- **Lid:** slides in grooves, held by a stainless ball detent (no glued magnets).
- **Finish:** bead-blasted, clear undyed anodize, hot-water seal (not nickel acetate). No paint, powder coat, glue, seals or plastic.
- **Branding:** laser-engraved logo, plus day letters in the compartments.
- **Rough cost:** US$6–12 FOB at 500, about CAD $10–19 landed. Quote needed.

## Process
1. Send the spec or sketch.
2. The factory makes a CAD drawing and quotes.
3. Prototype: ~US$50–150, 1–2 weeks.
4. Production: MOQ ~100, ~30 days, then DDP air to Ontario.
