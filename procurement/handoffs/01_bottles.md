## Decision (Oct 14, with designer Yuliia Runo)
**Format: amber straight-sided jar.** It replaces the packer bottle recommendation. Rachel accepted the extra cost.

| SKU | Jar | Landed per jar (regular) |
|---|---|---|
| Balance | [Burch 4 oz, 58-400](https://www.burchbottle.com/4oz-Amber-Straight-Sided-Jar-58-400) | ~$1.32 |
| 60ct | [Burch 6 oz, 63-400](https://www.burchbottle.com/6oz-Amber-Straight-Sided-Jar-63-400) | ~$1.59 |
| 90ct | [SKS 8 oz, 70-400](https://www.sks-bottle.com/product/305037.html) | ~$1.96 |

- **Total about $4,400 (~$1.47 per jar), regular finish.** That's ~$0.32 per jar more than the packer (~$3,400).
- **Burch/SKS are only the cheapest if their jars are NOT US-made.** Then they're about tied with a China order for Balance + 60ct (~$4,410 vs ~$4,430), and they avoid the ~3-month wait. If they ARE US-made, the 50% surtax pushes Burch + SKS to ~$5,600. Ordering Balance + 60ct from China (90ct still local) brings that back to ~$4,700. Burch and SKS don't state origin, so get it in writing. China prices are listing estimates until an RFQ is quoted.
- **Finish is still open.** Yuliia will show a matte (frosted) concept and a glossy one, and Rachel picks after seeing them. Frosted runs ~$4,950 (~$1.65 per jar). It comes from China: 4 oz + 6 oz by sea (~3 months), 8 oz by DDP air (~5–7 weeks).
- **Sizes:** Balance uses the 4 oz. The new SKUs use either 6 oz + 8 oz, or one shared size. That's decided at production, and Yuliia is designing the label to work on all three.
  - A shared size only works as the 8 oz, and only if the 60ct is size 00 (~54% full). With size 0, a 60ct looks under half full (~41%) in an 8 oz, which breaks the half-full rule.
  - The 90ct doesn't fit a 6 oz in size 00, and it's tight in size 0.
- Three neck sizes means three cap sizes (58, 63 and 70-400) and three shrink-band sizes.
- The packer route (TricorBraun 120/150/200cc, ~$3,400, China glass, no surtax) is the fallback if the jars don't work out.

## Labels and design (Oct 12–14)

  Rachel chooses one combination, then Yuliia develops it across all three products.
- **Timeline:** Yuliia needs 3–4 extra business days once packaging is confirmed. Rachel agreed.
- **What Yuliia needs from producers:** photos of the exact jars, finishes and label paper you'll actually be supplied, not website reference images. She also needs dielines to finalise label sizes.

## Key facts
- **Surtax (confirmed):** SOR/2026-186 puts a 50% surtax on US-ORIGIN glass jars (7010.90.00). It applies by country of origin, not where the jar ships from.
- **Duty:** China-origin glass is MFN Free.
- **Tamper evidence today:** cotton plus an outer plastic shrink band, with no heat seal.
- **Owner rules:**
  - No container may look less than half full. Balance in the 4 oz (~41%) is accepted.
  - No materials with potential endocrine disruptors (EDCs).
