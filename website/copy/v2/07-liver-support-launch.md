## Your liver already clears hormones every day

Your liver already clears hormones every day, along with alcohol, caffeine and many medications; Liver Support supplies nutrients that support that normal function.† It changes used hormones such as estrogen into forms your body can remove. Cocoon's formulas are built around 2 breakout patterns, high estrogen and high testosterone, and liver health supports either. That's why Liver Support is an add-on.

No liver supplement, ours included, has strong evidence for acne on its own. Liver Support isn't for liver disease: yellowing skin or eyes, dark urine or abnormal liver tests need a doctor. Learn [how your body clears excess estrogen](/blogs/learn/liver-health-and-acne).

## Liver Support is for women who want to support their liver's everyday work

Liver Support is for women 18 and over with hormonal acne who want to support their liver's everyday work. Your liver processes alcohol, caffeine and many medications. It may suit you if your routine includes:

1. Alcohol
2. Caffeine
3. A diet high in processed food and sugar

## Your Liver Support questions, answered

Your Liver Support questions, from pairing and R-ALA to medications and shipping, are answered here in 2 or 3 sentences each.

### What does Liver Support do?

It supplies 5 ingredients that support the liver's everyday work of clearing used hormones:† milk thistle, schisandra, dandelion, burdock and R-alpha lipoic acid. It contains no organ meat, and it isn't for liver disease.

### Do I need Liver Support and my core formula together?

No. Estrogen Balance or Androgen Balance is your core formula, matched to your breakout pattern, and Liver Support is an optional add-on. Start the core formula first. [NEED: how many weeks before adding Liver Support]

### How do I take Liver Support?

Take one capsule twice a day. One bottle of 60 capsules lasts 30 days. [NEED: with food or without, morning or evening]

### Why R-alpha lipoic acid instead of alpha lipoic acid?

Standard alpha lipoic acid is usually an equal mix of R and S forms. R-ALA is the R form alone, the form your body makes. [NEED: naturopath's reason for choosing it at 100 mg]

### Can I take Liver Support with birth control or other medications?

Talk to your doctor or pharmacist first about any prescription, including the pill, a hormonal IUD or HRT. Ask in particular about milk thistle, R-ALA if you take blood-sugar medication, and dandelion if you take diuretics or lithium.

### Will Liver Support make me break out at first?

A breakout isn't a sign it's working. New or deeper cysts in new areas, a rash, or skin still worse after 6 to 8 weeks means stop and contact us.

### How long should I take Liver Support?

About 3 cycles (about 3 months), then a check-in and a decision to taper or continue. [NEED: one sentence on pausing or cancelling a subscription, once terms are confirmed]
