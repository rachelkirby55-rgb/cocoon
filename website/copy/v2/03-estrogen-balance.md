## Is Estrogen Balance right for your breakouts? {#right-for-you}

Estrogen Balance is right for your breakouts if they arrive before your period, along your chin and jaw, like clockwork. Women describe the same spot every month: deep, painful cysts that never come to a head. Some also notice tender breasts or bloating before their period. Our guide covers the [signs of high estrogen and estrogen dominance](/blogs/learn/estrogen-dominance-and-acne).

If your breakouts started after you stopped the pill or got a hormonal IUD, or the pill once cleared your skin, take [the hormonal acne quiz](/pages/quiz) first. Those patterns often point to androgens.

It also fits after a baby, once breastfeeding has ended, and cycle-linked flares in your late 30s or 40s. If you're on HRT or your periods have stopped, ask your doctor first.

Dermatologists prescribe spironolactone and some combined birth control pills for hormonal acne in women, according to the [American Academy of Dermatology](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/acne/derm-treat/hormonal-therapy). Estrogen Balance is for women who can't take them, don't want them or have stopped.

This table sorts the two groups.

| They sit on your chin and jaw | They started with a hormonal IUD |
| The same spot flares every cycle | The pill once cleared your skin |
| Topicals haven't changed the pattern | Breakouts on your body, with oily skin |

If the left column fits, Estrogen Balance is a match. If anything on the right fits, let the quiz check first.

## Has DIM been studied for acne? {#evidence}

DIM hasn't been studied in an acne trial yet: the [2024 JCAD review of acne-supplement ingredients](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10941853/) found none. Here's what is known about how it works.

DIM forms from compounds in broccoli and other cruciferous vegetables. It changes how your body breaks down estrogen, according to Memorial Sloan Kettering. Calcium D-glucarate supports the gut's part, the normal clearance of used estrogen.† Our explainer covers [what DIM is and what the research shows](/blogs/learn/what-is-dim).

Glutathione is an antioxidant your liver makes. Its acne evidence is limited, and how well the body absorbs it by mouth is debated. Estrogen Balance uses S-acetylglutathione: 200 mg, giving 176 mg L-glutathione.

A mechanism isn't proof of clearer skin. That's why we ask you to give it about 3 cycles, follow a stop rule and lean on the guarantee.
