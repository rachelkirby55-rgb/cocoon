# High testosterone and acne: how androgens affect your skin

High testosterone in women can show as breakouts along the jaw, chin, neck or back, oily skin, chin hair or cycles over 35 days. Testosterone, DHT and DHEA-S are all androgens, and your oil glands can react to them even when blood tests come back normal.

## What are the signs of high testosterone in women? {#signs}

The signs of high testosterone in women show up in 3 places: your skin, your hair and your cycle. One sign alone says little; 2 or 3 together point to an androgen pattern.

Doctors call this pattern hyperandrogenism. The table sets out the common signs and why androgens cause each one.

**Signs of high testosterone in women**

| Where you notice it | What it looks like | Why androgens cause it |
|---|---|---|
| Skin | Deep, tender bumps on the jaw, chin, neck, chest or back | Androgens enlarge oil glands, and those zones have many of them |
| Skin | Oilier skin, especially through the day | Androgens push oil glands to make more oil |
| Skin | Dark, velvety skin on the neck or in skin folds | A clue to high insulin, which pushes testosterone up |
| Hair | Coarse hair on the chin, upper lip, chest or belly | Androgens turn fine hair into thicker, darker hair in these areas |
| Hair | Thinning at the crown of the scalp | Some scalp follicles shrink under androgens |
| Cycle | Cycles over 35 days, or hard to predict | High androgens can delay or skip ovulation |

If several rows fit, the androgen pattern is a reasonable place to start.

Three other patterns can look similar. Breakouts that start a few months after stopping the pill usually fit this androgen pattern. Premenstrual breakouts with tender breasts and a heavier flow fit a different pattern. Compare the [signs of high estrogen and estrogen dominance](/blogs/learn/estrogen-dominance-and-acne). Tiredness on its own doesn't point to high testosterone. [NEED: naturopath to confirm these three lines]

A deepening voice, or signs that appear within a few months, need a doctor soon. [Jump to the see-a-doctor list](#see-a-doctor).

## Androgens drive acne through your oil glands, even with normal labs {#how-androgens-work}

Androgens drive acne through your oil glands. When androgens reach them, the glands grow larger and make more oil. That's why your labs can look normal and your skin can still react.

Four androgens matter for skin: testosterone, DHT (dihydrotestosterone), DHEA-S and androstenedione. The chain runs in four steps. Androgens make oil glands produce more oil. The lining of the pore gets stickier and sheds less cleanly. Oil and cells clog the pore. Bacteria then multiply in the blocked pore, and inflammation follows.

**Why normal labs don't rule it out.** Some women's oil glands are more sensitive to normal amounts of testosterone and DHT. The skin also makes DHT inside the gland from testosterone, and a blood test can't see that. So a blood test can't show how much your skin converts or how strongly it responds.

A [2016 review of hormones in acne](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5015761/) explains both routes. It describes extra receptor sensitivity in the oil gland and higher activity of 5-alpha reductase, the enzyme that turns testosterone into DHT. It also notes that only about 1 to 2% of testosterone circulates free. Low sex hormone-binding globulin (SHBG) leaves more testosterone free, even when your total looks normal.

[IMAGE: Diagram. Left: insulin acting on the ovaries, which make more testosterone. Centre: the liver making less SHBG, so more testosterone stays free. Right: an oil gland turning testosterone into DHT, with more oil coming out. Alt text: "Diagram of insulin, SHBG and DHT working together to increase oil in the skin".]

The combined pill works on this chain from the other end. Its estrogen raises SHBG, which binds more testosterone, and it quiets the androgens your ovaries make.

Women need some testosterone. Made by the ovaries and adrenal glands, it supports sex drive, mood, energy, and bone and muscle strength. Much of it becomes estrogen. [NEED: naturopath to confirm]

## What causes high testosterone in women? {#causes}

High testosterone in women has a few common causes: insulin, PCOS (now also called PMOS), birth control changes, stress, life stages and some medicines.

PCOS is the best-known cause, and it gets [its own section below](#pcos). Rare causes, such as adrenal or ovarian tumours, are covered in the [see-a-doctor section](#see-a-doctor).

### Insulin pushes testosterone up

Insulin pushes testosterone up in two ways. High insulin tells the ovaries to make more testosterone. It also lowers SHBG from the liver, so more of that testosterone stays free to reach your skin. This happens in lean women too, not only in women with a higher weight. Dark, velvety skin on the neck is one outward clue. Your liver makes SHBG, so read more on [how your liver handles hormones](/blogs/learn/liver-health-and-acne). <!-- switch on January 2027 --> Your doctor can check your blood sugar if this sounds familiar.

### Stopping the pill can trigger an androgen rebound

Stopping the pill can trigger an androgen rebound, often a few months later. Combined pills raise SHBG and quiet your ovaries' androgens, so your skin gets used to low androgen activity. When you stop, SHBG falls and your ovaries restart. Pills with a progestin that blocks androgens, such as Yaz, Yasmin and Diane-35, can make the contrast sharper. Many women notice jawline breakouts 3 to 6 months after their last pill [CHECK: source for the 3 to 6 month timing]. Our guide to [acne after stopping birth control](/blogs/learn/acne-after-stopping-birth-control) covers how long it tends to last and what helps.

### Progestins in hormonal IUDs and the minipill can act like androgens on skin

Progestins in hormonal IUDs and the minipill can act like androgens on skin. Levonorgestrel, the progestin in most hormonal IUDs, is one example. Some women notice new breakouts in the months after insertion. You don't need to remove your IUD to work on your skin. Talk to your doctor about your options, and track your breakouts so you can see whether they follow your cycle. Our guide to [acne with a hormonal IUD](/blogs/learn/hormonal-iud-acne) goes into more detail. <!-- switch on February 2027 -->

### Stress can raise adrenal androgens

The adrenal glands make androgens such as DHEA-S, and long-running stress may nudge them up. Research on this link in acne is limited, though. A [2020 study of adult female acne](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7413461/) looked at stress among other triggers and hormone results. Stress also tends to cut sleep and change how you eat, which affects insulin. So stress works on androgens directly and through blood sugar. Steady sleep, regular meals and movement you enjoy help on both fronts. If your breakouts flare in your busiest weeks, note it in your tracking.

### Perimenopause and HRT can make testosterone's effect stronger

Perimenopause and HRT can make testosterone's effect stronger. In your 40s, estrogen starts to fall and swing, while testosterone declines more slowly. That leaves androgens with relatively more influence on your oil glands. Some HRT routines include testosterone, and some progestins in HRT can act like androgens. If acne arrives with irregular periods in your 40s, it may be [perimenopause acne](/blogs/learn/perimenopause-acne). <!-- switch on February 2027 --> After birth, and for as long as breastfeeding keeps periods away, estrogen levels stay low. That can leave androgens with more influence too. Supplements aren't for breastfeeding, so read our guide to [postpartum acne](/blogs/learn/postpartum-acne) for options that suit this stage. <!-- switch on April 2027 -->

### Some medicines, supplements and inherited conditions add androgens

Some medicines, supplements and inherited conditions add androgens. DHEA supplements, testosterone gel and anabolic steroids all raise androgen levels, according to [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/007622.htm). Testosterone gel can even transfer from a partner's skin. Nonclassic congenital adrenal hyperplasia (CAH) is an inherited condition in which the adrenal glands make extra androgens. It's often mistaken for PCOS. A family history of excess hair growth or early acne can be a clue. Tell your doctor about every supplement you take. Cocoon's formulas contain no DHEA.

## How do you know if your acne is PCOS (now also called PMOS)? {#pcos}

Knowing if your acne is PCOS (now also called PMOS) takes a doctor's assessment. Acne alone isn't enough, and high androgens can happen without PCOS.

PMOS stands for polyendocrine metabolic ovarian syndrome, the new name agreed by a [2026 global consensus published in The Lancet](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/42119588). The [2023 international PCOS guideline](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10505534/) uses the criteria in the table. You need 2 of the 3, after other causes are ruled out.

| Criterion | What it means |
|---|---|
| Irregular ovulation | Cycles over 35 days, fewer than about 8 periods a year, or no periods |
| High androgens | Signs such as acne or excess hair, or high androgens on a blood test |
| Polycystic ovaries | Many small follicles on an ultrasound. In adults, an AMH blood test can stand in for the ultrasound |
| Ruled out first | Thyroid problems, high prolactin, nonclassic CAH and Cushing's syndrome |

The "ruled out first" row matters, because each of those conditions can mimic PCOS. A quiz can point you to the androgen pattern, but only a doctor can confirm PCOS. [CHECK: "fewer than about 8 periods a year" is not in the brief; confirm against the guideline or cut]

### What does PCOS acne look like?

PCOS acne tends to be deep and inflamed. A [2025 review of skin signs in PCOS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12055046/) places it on the lower face, jawline, chest and back. It often comes with oily skin, and it can carry on well past your teens.

### Can you have high androgens without PCOS?

Yes. Insulin, coming off the pill, progestins, stress, nonclassic CAH and some medicines can all raise androgens without PCOS. Unwanted hair growth can also happen without PCOS. Conditions that cause pelvic pain can overlap with skin changes too; see [whether endometriosis causes acne](/blogs/learn/does-endometriosis-cause-adult-acne).

### Do supplements help PCOS acne?

Supplements have more evidence for PCOS cycles and insulin than for PCOS acne. Inositol is the most studied. The 2023 guideline says "Inositol (in any form) could be considered in women with PCOS based on individual preferences and values." It notes limited benefit for ovulation, excess hair or weight. Berberine and NAC have smaller trials. Spearmint trials measured testosterone. Few of these studies measured acne at all.

## An androgen panel needs more than total testosterone {#panel}

An androgen panel needs more than total testosterone: free testosterone, SHBG and DHEA-S catch what total testosterone misses.

The 2023 PCOS guideline recommends total and free testosterone first. Ask about these tests:

- Total and free testosterone
- SHBG, usually measured alongside testosterone so the lab can work out your free testosterone
- DHEA-S and androstenedione
- LH and FSH
- Thyroid (TSH), prolactin and 17-hydroxyprogesterone, if your doctor wants to rule out other causes

Your doctor may also check your blood sugar, with fasting glucose, HbA1c or a glucose tolerance test. Routine insulin tests aren't recommended by the 2023 international guideline. Here's more on [which hormone tests to ask for](/blogs/learn/hormone-tests-for-acne). <!-- switch on February 2027 -->

Canadian labs report testosterone in nmol/L, and US labs use ng/dL. Results taken on the combined pill don't show your own androgen levels. The guideline suggests stopping the pill for at least 3 months if testing is essential, with other contraception meanwhile.

Normal results don't rule out an androgen pattern. If your doctor only tests total testosterone, ask whether free testosterone and SHBG can be added.

## How can you support healthy testosterone levels naturally? {#natural-support}

Supporting healthy testosterone levels naturally starts with blood sugar: high insulin can raise testosterone, and meals, movement and sleep all affect insulin.

### Blood sugar and sleep come first

Blood sugar and sleep come first because they act on insulin every day. Build meals around protein, fibre and vegetables, and pair carbs with protein. Move after meals, even with a 10-minute walk [CHECK: source for the 10-minute walk]. Strength training helps your muscles use glucose. Aim for 7 to 9 hours of sleep, since short sleep makes insulin work harder [CHECK: source for sleep and insulin].

## When should you see a doctor about high testosterone? {#see-a-doctor}

You should see a doctor about high testosterone soon if your signs appear or worsen within a few months, or your voice deepens.

> **See a doctor if you notice:**
> - Signs that appear or worsen quickly, over a few months
> - A deeper voice
> - Balding at the temples or crown
> - New excess hair or acne after menopause
> - Periods that have stopped, if you aren't pregnant
> - Dark, velvety patches of skin
> - Deep cysts that leave scars
> - Any of these signs while you're trying to conceive

A doctor can rule out the rarer causes: nonclassic CAH, adrenal or ovarian tumours, ovarian hyperthecosis, Cushing's syndrome, and thyroid or prolactin problems. The [Endocrine Society hirsutism guideline](https://academic.oup.com/jcem/article/103/4/1233/4924418) sets out how doctors assess excess androgens.

### Prescriptions work for many women

Prescriptions work for many women, and they have the strongest evidence. Spironolactone is an oral prescription that reduces the effect of androgens on oil glands. It's judged after about 3 months and isn't used in pregnancy. Combined pills raise SHBG and quiet ovarian androgens. Winlevi (clascoterone) is a prescription cream available in the US and Canada. Metformin can help when insulin is high. Isotretinoin is an option for severe or scarring acne.

Supplements are one option for women who can't take spironolactone, don't want to, or have stopped it. Never stop or change a prescription without your prescriber. ## Questions women ask about high testosterone {#faq}

### Does testosterone cause acne?

Yes. Androgens make oil glands larger and more active. Your skin turns testosterone into DHT, so sensitive skin can break out even with normal blood levels.

### Does DHT cause acne?

Yes. DHT is the strongest androgen on oil glands, and the skin makes it from testosterone. So acne can track your skin's sensitivity more than a blood test. [NEED: naturopath to confirm]

### How can you tell if your acne is caused by androgens?

Look at the whole pattern: breakouts on the jaw, chin, neck or body, plus oily skin, chin hair or cycles over 35 days. Normal labs don't rule it out.

### Is acne on your back and chest hormonal too?

It can be, when your chest and back flare the same week as your jawline. Sweat, friction and yeast also cause body acne, so mention the timing to your doctor.

### If a DIM supplement didn't help after 3 months, could your acne be androgen-led?

It could be. The 2024 JCAD review found no acne trials of DIM. DIM's role is in how the body processes estrogen, so it may not suit an androgen pattern. Take the quiz to see which pattern fits.

### Can you combine supplements with spironolactone, the pill, metformin or a GLP-1?

Talk to your doctor or pharmacist first. Berberine can add to the blood-sugar effects of metformin and GLP-1 medicines [CHECK: MSKCC names diabetes medicines generally, not metformin or GLP-1s]. Saw palmetto may interact with hormonal contraception [CHECK: source; NCCIH doesn't mention it]. Never change a prescription dose. [NEED: interactions table]

### Can you take androgen-focused supplements on testosterone therapy or HRT?

Ask your prescriber first. Testosterone therapy raises androgens on purpose, so androgen-balance ingredients may work against it. HRT can also change which pattern fits.

### What happens if testosterone is too high in women?

Mostly skin, hair and cycle effects, and conception can be harder. Insulin resistance raises the risk of type 2 diabetes. Rarely, an adrenal or ovarian tumour is the cause and needs prompt care.

### What blocks DHT naturally?

Saw palmetto and spearmint are the ingredients most often studied for DHT or testosterone, and the evidence in women is thin. Saw palmetto has mostly been studied in men. No food or supplement has good evidence for clearing DHT-linked acne in women.

### What are the signs of PCOS?

Beyond acne, the common signs are irregular or missed periods, extra hair on the face or body, scalp thinning and weight changes. Diagnosis needs a doctor.

### Can insulin resistance or blood sugar spikes cause acne?

They can add to it. High insulin raises testosterone and lowers SHBG, so more androgen reaches your oil glands and oil rises. [NEED: naturopath to confirm] [NEED: links to the diet and berberine articles; the berberine link is already used in the table above]

### Which supplements and vitamins are studied for high testosterone?

Spearmint, zinc, inositol, berberine, NAC and saw palmetto. Each has small studies at best, and few measured acne. The graded table above rates each one. [NEED: naturopath to confirm] [NEED: URL of the Best Supplements page]

### How do I get rid of acne caused by testosterone or DHT?

Start with a doctor for prescription options, such as spironolactone, a combined pill or Winlevi. Support blood sugar with meals, movement and sleep. Supplements fit in for women who can't or won't take a prescription. Read more on [how to get rid of hormonal acne](/blogs/learn/how-to-get-rid-of-hormonal-acne). [NEED: naturopath to confirm]
