## Your liver already clears hormones every day

Your liver already clears hormones every day, along with alcohol, caffeine and many medications; Liver Support supplies nutrients that support that normal function.† It changes used hormones such as estrogen into forms your body can remove.

Cocoon's formulas are built around 2 breakout patterns: high estrogen and high testosterone. Liver health supports either. No liver supplement, ours included, has strong evidence for acne on its own, so Liver Support is an optional extra, not a core formula.

Liver Support isn't for liver disease. Yellowing skin or eyes, dark urine or abnormal liver tests need a doctor. Learn [how your body clears excess estrogen](/blogs/learn/liver-health-and-acne).

- Alcohol
- Caffeine (your liver processes it, along with alcohol and many medications)
- A diet high in processed food and sugar

Skip Liver Support if you're pregnant, breastfeeding or under 18, and ask your doctor or pharmacist first about medications, supplements or a daisy-family allergy.
