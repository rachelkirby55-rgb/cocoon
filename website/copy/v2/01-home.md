## What do women ask before they start?

Women ask about medications before anything else, so that answer comes first.

### Can I take Cocoon with birth control, spironolactone or other medication?
Ask your doctor or pharmacist first, and bring the label. Flag DIM and saw palmetto with hormonal birth control, saw palmetto with blood thinners, and berberine with diabetes medicines. Don't change your spironolactone dose without your prescriber. There's more in [Cocoon's FAQ on medications and birth control](/pages/faq#birth-control).

### Do supplements for hormonal acne work?
The evidence is uneven. A [2024 review in the Journal of Clinical and Aesthetic Dermatology](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10941853/) found no acne trials of DIM. Most Estrogen Balance reviewers who mention timing noticed a change at about 3-6 weeks [NEED: count]. Judge any formula over about 3 cycles.

### Is zinc good for hormonal acne?
Zinc is among the better-studied supplements for inflammatory acne, according to a [2023 systematic review in JAMA Dermatology](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11015159/). Many of its trials were small. High doses can cause side effects, so check your total with a pharmacist. Androgen Balance (January 2027) has 20 mg a day. Here's [how much zinc is safe for acne](/blogs/learn/zinc-for-acne).

### Is there a pill to stop hormonal acne?
Yes, by prescription. Spironolactone and some combined birth control pills are [prescription options for hormonal acne](/blogs/learn/prescription-acne-medications). The [American Academy of Dermatology's acne guidelines](https://www.aad.org/news/updated-guidelines-acne-management) back both. Cocoon is for women who can't or won't take those, or have stopped.

### Can I take Cocoon while pregnant, trying to conceive or breastfeeding?
No. Cocoon isn't for pregnancy, trying to conceive, breastfeeding or anyone under 18. After a baby, consider it once breastfeeding has ended, with your doctor's OK. Your doctor can suggest options until then.

Yes. Cocoon ships to Canada and the US, priced in CAD and USD. Estrogen Balance ships from Canada, and Androgen Balance and Liver Support will ship from the US. [NEED: US delivery time, free-shipping threshold and currency] See [Canada and US shipping times and duties](/pages/shipping-policy).
