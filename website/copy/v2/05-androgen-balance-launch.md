## Who is Androgen Balance for?

Androgen Balance is for adult women whose breakouts follow the pattern linked to high testosterone and other androgens. Look for these skin signs:

- Breakouts along your jawline and chin
- Breakouts on your chest, back or shoulders
- Oily skin
- Breakouts that began after you stopped the pill, or with a hormonal IUD

Read [how high testosterone affects your skin](/blogs/learn/high-testosterone-and-acne).

**It's not for you if** you're pregnant, trying to conceive, breastfeeding or under 18. On testosterone therapy or HRT? Ask your prescriber before starting.

After a baby, start only once you've finished breastfeeding. Jawline flares in perimenopause follow the same skin pattern. [NEED: naturopath to confirm the perimenopause line]

### Berberine is the formula's anchor ingredient

Berberine, the formula's anchor ingredient, has been studied for its effects on hormone markers in a few early studies. Acne trials are few. [NEED: naturopath's one-line reason berberine is the anchor, without blood-sugar, insulin or weight wording] Read more on [how berberine affects blood sugar and skin](/blogs/learn/berberine-for-acne).

### Saw palmetto supports healthy androgen balance†

Saw palmetto has been studied for how it affects the conversion of testosterone to DHT, mostly in men and on hair. Here's [what saw palmetto does in women](/blogs/learn/saw-palmetto-for-women).

### Zinc supports healthy skin†

Zinc has the most acne research of the five, according to a 2024 dermatology review. A serving gives you 20 mg of elemental zinc, half the 40 mg adult daily upper limit. Adult women need about 8 mg a day. Too much for too long can lower your copper levels, and the formula doesn't include copper. See [how much zinc is safe for acne](/blogs/learn/zinc-for-acne).

### Sarsaparilla supports a healthy stress response†

Our naturopath included sarsaparilla root for stress-response support. There's little modern human research on it. Learn [what sarsaparilla root does](/blogs/learn/sarsaparilla-root).

### NAC supports the liver's natural clearance pathways†

NAC is a building block for glutathione, an antioxidant the body makes. It hasn't been well tested for acne, and some people break out after starting it. More on [NAC's benefits and side effects for acne](/blogs/learn/nac-for-acne).

## Take three capsules a day, and judge it at about 3 cycles

Take three capsules of Androgen Balance a day with food, and judge it at about three cycles (about 3 months) before deciding whether to taper or keep going.

1. **Take it as the label says.** One capsule, three times a day, with food. Take it a few hours apart from other medicines and supplements. One bottle lasts 30 days.
2. **Expect an adjustment period.** Some people have stomach upset in the first weeks. Some break out after starting berberine or NAC.
3. **Know the stop rule.** New or deeper cysts in new areas, a rash, or skin still worse after 6 to 8 weeks means stop and contact Cocoon. Stop and talk to your doctor about mood changes or any severe reaction.
4. **Check in at about 3 cycles.** Then taper or keep going. The label asks you to check with a healthcare practitioner before using it beyond 3 months. Read [how long acne supplements take to work](/blogs/learn/how-long-do-acne-supplements-take-to-work).

## What if you've tried spironolactone or the pill?

If you've tried spironolactone or the pill, you've tried what dermatologists most often recommend for this pattern, and many women do well on them. The same goes for retinoids and isotretinoin.

Androgen Balance is for women who can't take them, for example because of low blood pressure or side effects. It's also for women who'd rather not, or who stopped and saw breakouts return. Never stop or change a prescription without your prescriber. To take both, check the medication table with your doctor or pharmacist first.
