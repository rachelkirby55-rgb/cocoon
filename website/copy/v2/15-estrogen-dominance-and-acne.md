# Estrogen Dominance and Acne: Signs of High Estrogen and What Helps

Estrogen dominance means estrogen is high compared with progesterone, from excess estrogen or low progesterone, and it's a pattern, not a diagnosis. There's no agreed lab cut-off, but women who feel estrogen dominant often notice tender breasts, heavier periods and breakouts before their period. Estrogen itself lowers oil, so the skin link is about timing and balance.

## What is estrogen dominance?

Estrogen dominance (also spelled oestrogen dominance) is a pattern in which estrogen runs high relative to progesterone. It describes a ratio and a cluster of symptoms, with no agreed lab cut-off.

You'll also see it called high estrogen, excess estrogen or too much estrogen. On this page, "estrogen dominance" means the pattern and "high estrogen" means your actual levels.

[IMAGE: Diagram, two-route see-saw. Left route: estrogen rises while progesterone stays normal. Right route: estrogen stays normal while progesterone drops. Both tip the see-saw towards estrogen. Alt text: "See-saw diagram showing two routes to estrogen dominance: high estrogen or low progesterone".]

### High estrogen and low progesterone both tip the ratio

High estrogen and low progesterone both tip the ratio the same way. In the first route, estrogen climbs while progesterone stays normal. In the second, estrogen is normal but progesterone runs low, usually because you skipped ovulation that cycle. You only make real amounts of progesterone after you ovulate, so a cycle without ovulation leaves estrogen unopposed.

### "Estrogen dominant" is a contested label for a recognizable pattern

"Estrogen dominant" is a contested label because mainstream medicine doesn't diagnose it. [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/22363-high-estrogen) notes that some healthcare providers use the term, while most doctors assess the same symptoms as PMS, heavy periods or perimenopause. The cluster is still real and recognizable, so it's useful for spotting which hormones to ask about.

## The signs of high or excess estrogen follow your cycle {#signs}

The signs of high or excess estrogen cluster in the week or two before your period. One sign alone means little; tender breasts, a heavier period and chin breakouts together are the pattern.

[IMAGE: Bar chart of Cocoon's 2026 quiz answers: share of respondents reporting each sign below. Title includes source and year, e.g. "Signs reported in the Cocoon quiz, 2026 (n = [NEED])". Alt text: "Bar chart of premenstrual signs reported by Cocoon quiz takers in 2026". [NEED: quiz export]]

Here's how each sign lines up with estrogen, and what else could explain it.

| Sign | When it shows up | Why estrogen is linked | Could also be |
|---|---|---|---|
| Breast tenderness | The week or two before your period | Estrogen stimulates breast tissue | Normal cycle changes |
| Heavy, long or painful periods | During your period | Estrogen thickens the uterine lining | Fibroids, endometriosis, a bleeding disorder |
| PMS mood changes | 1 to 2 weeks before your period | Estrogen and progesterone both affect mood chemistry | PMDD, depression, anxiety, stress |
| Bloating | Before your period | Hormone shifts change fluid balance | Diet, constipation, food intolerance |
| Period headaches | Just before or during your period | Headaches can follow the drop in estrogen | Migraine, dehydration, poor sleep |
| Weight gain on hips and thighs | Gradual | Fat tissue makes estrogen | Diet, activity, age, thyroid |
| Poor sleep or fatigue | Often worse before your period | Progesterone supports sleep, so low progesterone can disturb it | Iron deficiency, thyroid, stress |
| Premenstrual breakouts | The week before your period | Estrogen and progesterone fall while androgens hold steady | Androgens, especially with oily skin or irregular cycles |

The table works best as a tracking guide. Note your signs for 2 to 3 cycles before you draw a conclusion. If the cluster appeared after you stopped the pill, it may simply be normal ovulatory cycles returning, with their natural highs and lows.

### Low estrogen looks different

Low estrogen looks different: hot flashes, night sweats, vaginal dryness, and light or missed periods. These signs point the other way, so this page's advice isn't for low estrogen. If they sound like you, talk to your doctor.

| | High estrogen pattern | Low estrogen pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Periods | Heavier, longer, more painful | Lighter, irregular or missed |
| Breasts | Tender or swollen before your period | Rarely tender |
| Temperature | Usually unaffected | Hot flashes, night sweats |
| Skin | Breakouts before your period | Dryness |

## Can high estrogen cause acne? {#acne}

High estrogen doesn't cause acne on its own. Estrogen lowers oil production and ties up testosterone. Breakouts linked to this pattern come before your period, when estrogen and progesterone fall and androgens hold steady.

Estrogen ties up testosterone by raising sex hormone-binding globulin (SHBG). SHBG is a liver protein that binds testosterone, so less reaches your oil glands. [A 2016 review of hormones in acne](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5015761/) describes this SHBG effect and notes that estrogen's oil-lowering effect shows up at high doses. When estrogen falls before your period, androgens have relatively more influence on your skin.

Acne research measures individual hormones and doesn't define "estrogen dominance", so the link is an observed pattern, not a measured cause. [NEED: naturopath confirms this wording]

### Where do estrogen-pattern breakouts show up?

Estrogen-pattern breakouts show up on the chin and jaw, the same zones as androgen breakouts. Timing and company separate them. Estrogen-pattern spots arrive in the week before your period, alongside tender breasts or a heavier flow. Our guide to [breakouts before, during and after your period](/blogs/learn/period-acne) covers the timing in detail. <!-- switch on January 2027, when the period acne article goes live -->

There's no single "estrogen face". Estrogen is linked with melasma, the brown patches some women get in pregnancy or on the pill, but that's pigment, not acne.

[IMAGE: Face-zone illustration with chin and jawline highlighted, labelled for both estrogen-pattern and androgen-pattern breakouts. Alt text: "Face illustration highlighting the chin and jawline, where hormonal breakouts often appear".]

### Can low progesterone cause acne?

Low progesterone can play a part, because it lets estrogen and androgens swing more sharply. More progesterone isn't the answer, though. Some progestins, the synthetic forms in birth control, act like androgens on skin. Don't use progesterone cream unless a doctor prescribes it.

## Isn't estrogen good for skin? {#good-for-skin}

Estrogen is good for skin in most ways: it supports collagen and hydration and lowers oil. That's why combined birth control pills, which pair estrogen with a progestin, clear acne for many women.

The pill clears skin in three ways. It pauses ovulation, raises SHBG and cuts the androgens your ovaries make. Some combined pills are approved specifically for acne. [CHECK: name a source for FDA acne approval of specific pills; the 2016 review doesn't list them]

If the pill cleared your skin and breakouts returned after you stopped, androgens are the likelier driver. Our guide to [acne after stopping birth control](/blogs/learn/acne-after-stopping-birth-control) explains why.

The issue is estrogen relative to progesterone, and the swings around your period. Estrogen itself isn't bad.

## Estrogen dominance has three common causes {#causes}

Estrogen dominance has three common causes. You ovulate less often, so you make less progesterone; you make or take in more estrogen; or you clear estrogen more slowly.

[IMAGE: Three-route causes diagram: (1) less ovulation, less progesterone; (2) more estrogen made or taken in; (3) slower clearance through the liver and gut. Alt text: "Diagram of the three common causes of estrogen dominance".]

### You ovulate less often

You ovulate less often in a few common situations. In perimenopause, progesterone often falls first, which is one reason some women see [acne that returns in perimenopause](/blogs/learn/perimenopause-acne). <!-- switch on February 2027 --> Stress, under-eating and very heavy training can also pause ovulation. So can the first cycles after stopping the pill or weaning a baby.

### You make or take in more estrogen

You make or take in more estrogen through a few routes. Body fat makes estrogen, which is biology, not blame. Alcohol raises blood estrogen levels, according to the [National Cancer Institute](https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/causes-prevention/risk/alcohol/alcohol-fact-sheet). Hormone replacement therapy (HRT) adds estrogen directly. Some everyday chemicals, such as BPA from some plastics and can linings, may interfere with the body's hormones. How much this matters for skin hasn't been well studied.

Conditions that involve estrogen can overlap with skin changes too. Read more on [whether endometriosis causes acne](/blogs/learn/does-endometriosis-cause-adult-acne).

### You clear estrogen more slowly

You clear estrogen more slowly when your gut moves slowly. Your liver packages used estrogen for removal, and it leaves mostly through your stool. Constipation and low fibre slow that exit. A gut enzyme, beta-glucuronidase, can unpack some estrogen so it's reabsorbed instead.

One more cause to rule out: acne that starts on a levonorgestrel IUD is usually related to the progestin, not estrogen.

## No single test confirms estrogen dominance {#tests}

No single test confirms estrogen dominance, because your ratio changes daily. The most useful picture combines 2-3 cycles of symptom tracking with blood tests about 7 days after ovulation.

## What helps with estrogen dominance? {#what-helps}

What helps estrogen dominance is mostly what supports regular cycles and clearance: more fibre, less alcohol and steady sleep. Heavy periods deserve a doctor's check, and supplements come after the basics.

### Eat more fibre and drink less alcohol

Eating more fibre and drinking less alcohol are the two most direct changes. [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/002470.htm) puts the adult fibre target at 21 to 38 grams a day. Add it gradually over a few weeks. Broccoli, kale and cauliflower add fibre and cruciferous compounds. Here's more on [how your body clears excess estrogen](/blogs/learn/liver-health-and-acne). <!-- switch on January 2027 --> Food triggers matter to many women as well, so see [sugar, dairy and the hormonal acne diet](/blogs/learn/hormonal-acne-diet). <!-- switch on March 2027 -->

[IMAGE: Cruciferous vegetables and lentils on a kitchen counter. Alt text: "Broccoli, kale, cauliflower and lentils, high-fibre foods".]

### Sleep well, eat enough and manage stress

Sleeping well, eating enough and managing stress all support regular ovulation. Adults need 7 to 9 hours of sleep a night, according to the [National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/sleep/how-much-sleep). Under-eating and long stretches of stress can pause ovulation, which lowers progesterone.

### Ask your doctor about prescription options

Prescription options work well for many women. For heavy periods, doctors may suggest anti-inflammatories, tranexamic acid or a hormonal IUD. For acne, options include a topical retinoid, benzoyl peroxide, azelaic acid, spironolactone or a combined pill.

> **See a doctor if you have:**
> - Heavy bleeding
> - Missed periods, if you aren't pregnant
> - A new breast lump
> - Pelvic pain

## Questions women ask about estrogen dominance {#faq}

### How do you get rid of estrogen acne?

Check the pattern first. Premenstrual breakouts with tender breasts or heavier periods fit estrogen. Oily skin or irregular cycles point to androgens. Start with fibre, less alcohol and sleep, and judge any supplement over about 3 cycles.

### Can I take DIM with birth control, a hormonal IUD or HRT?

Ask your doctor or pharmacist first, and bring the label so they can see the dose per capsule. [NEED: naturopath-signed interactions table]

### Does DIM lower estrogen too much?

There isn't enough research to say. DIM mainly changes how estrogen is broken down. If you have hot flashes or missed periods, or you take HRT, ask your doctor first. [NEED: naturopath to confirm]

### Does birth control cause estrogen dominance?

There's no good evidence that the pill leaves lasting estrogen dominance. Ovulation and its natural swings return when you stop. Breakouts that start a few months later are more often linked to androgens.

### Is estrogen dominance more common in perimenopause?

Often, yes. In your late 30s and 40s, progesterone often falls first and estrogen can swing higher. If you're on HRT, ask your prescriber how the doses fit together.

### Does estrogen dominance cause weight gain?

It can, and the link runs both ways, because fat tissue also makes estrogen. Weight gain alone doesn't point to estrogen dominance. For quick, unexplained changes, ask your doctor.

### Can high estrogen affect fertility?

It can, mainly through irregular ovulation. Endometriosis and fibroids can also affect fertility. If you're trying to conceive, see your doctor before any supplement that affects hormone metabolism.

### Is PMDD caused by estrogen dominance?

Not on current evidence. Research links PMDD to sensitivity to normal hormone changes. If mood before your period disrupts work or relationships, see your doctor, because effective options exist.

### Do I need less estrogen or more progesterone?

It's about the balance between the two. Estrogen can be high, progesterone can be low, or both can happen at once. [NEED: naturopath to confirm wording]

### Can high estrogen cause anxiety, mood swings, headaches or sore breasts?

Sore breasts: yes, often. Mood swings and anxiety: sometimes, mostly before your period. Headaches: sometimes, usually as estrogen drops. Bloating and constipation: sometimes. Nausea and bruising: rarely, so see your doctor if they're new. [NEED: naturopath to confirm each answer]

### Does ashwagandha increase estrogen?

There's little human research on ashwagandha and estrogen, so no one can say for sure. Cocoon's formulas don't contain it. [NEED: naturopath to confirm]

### What are the symptoms of low estrogen?

Low estrogen shows up as hot flashes, night sweats, vaginal dryness, and light or missed periods. See the high-versus-low table in the signs section above. Estrogen Balance isn't for low-estrogen signs.

### Are there natural estrogen blockers?

Not in the way the phrase suggests. Foods like broccoli, and ingredients like DIM and calcium D-glucarate, support how your body clears used estrogen. They don't stop estrogen. [NEED: naturopath to confirm]
