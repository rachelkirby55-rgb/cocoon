## Who is Androgen Balance for?

Androgen Balance is for adult women whose breakouts follow the pattern linked to high testosterone and other androgens. Look for these skin signs:

- Breakouts along your jawline and chin
- Breakouts on your chest, back or shoulders
- Oily skin
- Breakouts that began after you stopped the pill, or with a hormonal IUD

Read [how high testosterone affects your skin](/blogs/learn/high-testosterone-and-acne).

**It's not for you if** you're pregnant, trying to conceive, breastfeeding or under 18. If you're on testosterone therapy or HRT, ask your prescriber before starting. After a baby, wait until you've finished breastfeeding.

Do your breakouts flare before your period, with breast tenderness or bloating? That pattern fits [Estrogen Balance, a DIM supplement for women](/products/estrogen-balance), made with DIM, glutathione and calcium D-glucarate.

### Berberine is the formula's anchor ingredient

Berberine, the formula's anchor ingredient, has been studied for its effects on hormone markers in a few early studies. It hasn't been well tested for acne. [NEED: naturopath's one-line reason berberine is the anchor, without blood-sugar, insulin or weight wording]

### Saw palmetto supports healthy androgen balance†

Saw palmetto has been studied for how it affects the conversion of testosterone to DHT, mostly in lab studies and in men. It hasn't been tested for acne in women.

### Zinc supports healthy skin†

Zinc has the most acne research of the five, according to a 2024 dermatology review. A serving gives you 20 mg of elemental zinc, half the 40 mg adult daily upper limit. Adult women need about 8 mg a day. Large doses can upset your stomach, and too much for too long can lower your copper levels.

### Sarsaparilla supports a healthy stress response†

Our naturopath included sarsaparilla root for stress-response support. Skin research on it is thin.

### NAC supports the liver's natural clearance pathways†

NAC is a building block for glutathione, an antioxidant the body makes. It hasn't been well tested for acne.

## Give it about 3 cycles, and know when to stop

Give Androgen Balance about 3 cycles (about 3 months) before judging it, take it with food, and stop early if the stop rule applies.

- **Take it with food.** The label says one capsule, three times a day, with food. Berberine and zinc can upset an empty stomach.
- **Expect an adjustment period.** Some people get stomach upset in the first weeks, and some break out after starting berberine or NAC.
- **Know the stop rule.** New or deeper cysts in new areas, a rash, or skin still worse after 6 to 8 weeks means stop and contact Cocoon.
- **Call your doctor if needed.** Stop and talk to your doctor about mood changes or any severe reaction. Mention cycle or libido changes, too.
- **Check in at about 3 cycles.** Then taper or continue. The label asks you to check with a healthcare practitioner before using it beyond 3 months.

## What if you've tried spironolactone or the pill?

If you've tried spironolactone or the pill, you've tried what dermatologists most often recommend for this pattern, and many women do well on them. The same goes for retinoids and isotretinoin.

Androgen Balance is for women who can't take them, for example because of low blood pressure or side effects. It's also for women who'd rather not, or who stopped and saw breakouts return. Never stop or change a prescription without your prescriber. To take both, check the medication table with your doctor or pharmacist first.
