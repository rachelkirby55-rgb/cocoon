## Why does Cocoon cost more than a bottle of plain DIM?

Cocoon costs more than plain DIM because Estrogen Balance pairs DIM with glutathione and calcium D-glucarate, at disclosed doses. The [2024 JCAD review of acne-supplement ingredients](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10941853/) found no acne trials of DIM. Here's [what DIM is and what the research shows](/blogs/learn/what-is-dim).

DIM changes how the body breaks down estrogen, according to [Memorial Sloan Kettering](https://www.mskcc.org/cancer-care/integrative-medicine/herbs/diindolylmethane), and supports healthy estrogen metabolism.† Glutathione and calcium D-glucarate support how the body clears used estrogen.† Most Estrogen Balance reviewers who mention timing noticed a change at about 3-6 weeks [NEED: count]. Compared with a single-ingredient DIM capsule, you get three things:

That's about $2.27 CAD ($1.67 USD) a day on the 1-month supply, or $1.97 CAD ($1.45 USD) on the 3-month supply.

## What should you know before you buy?

Before you buy, check your medications: that answer comes first.

### Do any of Cocoon's formulas interact with medication?
Ask your doctor or pharmacist first. Estrogen Balance: hormonal birth control. Androgen Balance: metformin and other diabetes medicines, birth control and blood thinners; its label also rules out antibiotics and nitroglycerin. Liver Support: prescription and blood-sugar medicines. [NEED: naturopath-signed interactions table] More in [Cocoon's FAQ on medications and birth control](/pages/faq#birth-control).

### Do acne supplements work?
Some ingredients have trial data, and many don't; the 2024 JCAD review found no acne trials of DIM. Look for disclosed doses and no proprietary blend. Then judge over about 3 cycles. Our guide explains [how long acne supplements take to work](/blogs/learn/how-long-do-acne-supplements-take-to-work).

### Do hormone balancing supplements actually work?
Broad hormone blends have little direct acne evidence, and the phrase is vague. Cocoon names each pattern and dose instead. Estrogen Balance supports healthy estrogen metabolism.† Androgen Balance, launching January 2027, supports healthy testosterone levels.† Here's [the evidence on hormonal acne supplements](/blogs/learn/best-supplements-for-hormonal-acne).

### Can I take Estrogen Balance and Androgen Balance together?
Start with one, matched to your pattern, so you can tell what's working. The two share no active ingredients. The Androgen Balance label says to take it a few hours before or after other supplements. [NEED: formulator's answer on combining, before January 2027]
