1. **Raise the Canada target CPA from CA$84 to CA$105.** *Why:* at CA$84 Google is losing ~80% of searches to rank and spends CA$10–17 of the CA$50 a day. If you'd rather not spend at break-even, the other choice is leave it at CA$84 and accept ~1 sale a week. Budget stays CA$50/day (no change).

A DIM-specific ad (headline and text about DIM + glutathione) should convert better and give the Black Friday seasonality adjustment something to work on. *Expected:* more of the sales that already work. I'd do #2 one week after #1 so we can see what each did (max two big changes, and not at once).
3. **Add 3 negatives and 1 new phrase keyword (Canada).** Negatives (spent CA$6–13 each in Oct, 0 sales): "stella supplement", "new moon hormo balance", "noura nutrition". I'll check next week.
