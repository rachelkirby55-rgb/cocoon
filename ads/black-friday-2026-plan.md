# Black Friday 2026 plan (Google + Meta)

Sale: Monday Nov 23 to Cyber Monday Nov 30, 2026 (US Thanksgiving is Thu Nov 26, Black Friday Fri Nov 27). Drafted Oct 15, 2026. Nothing here is live until Rachel approves each step.

## What history says
- Black Friday 2024 on Meta: about CA$300 per sale. The Nov 2025 BFCM batch: no signal. BF auctions are crowded and expensive; cold audiences rarely pay off for a small brand.
- What did work: the Before/After carousel to warm audiences (CA$30 per sale, retargeting only).
- So: keep the always-on campaigns steady, spend the extra money on people who already know Cocoon, and lean on email/SMS (Klaviyo) for past customers.

## Decisions needed from Rachel (by Oct 30)
1. Offer: % off, and is it a **discount code** or a **price drop** in Shopify? (A price drop flows into Merchant Center and must match the Google price assets; a code doesn't.)
2. Does the sale apply to subscriptions, and to the US store?
3. Extra budget for the 8 days, on top of normal spend.

## Not doing
- No new prospecting campaign or broad cold test during BF (2024 showed ~CA$300 per sale).
- No editing the always-on ads to add sale text; that resets learning. Sale ads live in their own campaign.
- No Performance Max or Shopping "just for BF".
